Use this planner to estimate the cost of school meals, Acorns wraparound care and additional termly clubs for your children.

Choose the arrangements that apply to each child, then select the number of school weeks you would like to estimate.

🦱 Your Children

Add up to four children to include their individual arrangements in the estimate.

🍱 School Meals

School meals are charged at £3.55 per meal/day. Select the days each child requires a meal.

🏫 Acorns Wraparound Care

Select the appropriate session for each child on each day.

Breakfast Club: 07:30–08:45 = £5.00 including breakfast, or 08:15–08:45 = £3.00 without breakfast.

After-School Club: 15:15–17:00 = £10.00, or 15:15–18:00 = £12.00.
