## Introduction

I've recently been working on a small school costs planner designed to help parents estimate their weekly and termly school-related costs.

The idea was to make something that was simple enough to use quickly, while still being flexible enough to handle different arrangements for multiple children.

## The idea

The planner allows parents to enter:

- School meals
- Acorns breakfast club
- Acorns after-school club
- Additional termly clubs
- The number of school weeks

The calculator then produces an estimated total.
